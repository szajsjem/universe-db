# Molecular and compound crystal evidence

`sources/crystals/molecular.json` contains 11 experimental determinations and 37 asymmetric-unit sites, with corresponding untouched COD CIFs, revision identifiers and SHA-256 hashes. All records are pending human review.

- [Alpha quartz](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1011097.html), [aragonite](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9000229.html) and [high-temperature calcite](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9014344.html). Calcite conditions are exactly converted from source notes: 644 degrees Celsius = 917.15 K, 101 kPa = 101000 Pa.
- Deuterated [ice Ih](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1538597.html), [ice Ic](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1566634.html) and [ice II](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1566637.html). These are D2O specimens, not natural-H2O measurements. Hydrogen sites retain isotope mass number 2; disorder occupancies remain explicit.
- Alpha sulfur [1935 determination](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1011160.html) and [2002 determination](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1537087.html), with their distinct Fddd origin settings.
- [Black phosphorus](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9012486.html), [anhydrous calcium chloride](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1011280.html) and [molecular P4O10](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/2310555.html). Hydrated calcium chloride is not represented by the anhydrous record.

Coordinates preserve source decimal rounding, with coordinate standard uncertainties stored separately when available. The raw CIFs retain the authoritative symmetry operations and publication attribution. Coordinates are asymmetric-unit sites: they must be expanded through the stated setting before full-cell calculations. Missing occupancy, multiplicity, measurement temperature and pressure stay null. No room-temperature assumptions or natural-hydrogen density inference were introduced.

COD identifies these as public-domain/CC0 data; original publication authors should be acknowledged, and attribution notices from raw CIFs are retained.
